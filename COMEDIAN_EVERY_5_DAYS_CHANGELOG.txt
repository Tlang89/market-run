MARKET RUN v2.2 — THE COMEDIAN EVERY 5 DAYS

CHANGES
- The Comedian is guaranteed on in-game Days 5, 10, 15, 20, and every fifth day after that.
- He no longer appears randomly between scheduled appearances.
- The Comedian takes priority over Market Man, Donnie, and random street events on scheduled days.
- Staying, traveling, and taking a recovery day all count toward the five-day schedule.
- Normal street events still keep their existing cooldown.
- Added cache-busting to index.html so GitHub Pages loads the new rebuild.js.
- Added test URL support: append ?comedian=1 to trigger him immediately after starting or continuing.
