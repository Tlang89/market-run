MARKET MAN — v2.1 CHANGELOG
===========================

NEW CHARACTER
- Added Market Man character artwork: market_man.png
- Rare appearance about every 6–10 day advances
- Minimum five-day cooldown between appearances
- Saved appearance schedule survives browser reloads

CHOICES
1. HOT TIP
   - Calls an item and city for the next market move
   - 70%: 20–45% favorable spike
   - 20%: nearly flat result
   - 10%: 15–30% reversal/crash
   - Pending tips are saved

2. BACKROOM DEAL
   - Offers 3–5 units
   - 15–25% below the current local market
   - Buys only what cash and carrying space allow
   - 70% clean deal
   - 20% slight heat increase
   - 10% immediate 15–30% local market crash

3. SAFE PLAY
   - Finds the best current city-to-city spread
   - Protects the buy ceiling and sell floor
   - Protection lasts for two market moves
   - Protected routes are saved

RARE CHAOS CALL
- Optional "Plans Changed" encounter
- 70% creates a 25–50% local price spike
- 20% scrambles every local market
- 10% creates a 20–35% crash and adds heat

TESTING
Add ?marketman=1 to the live game URL. Start or continue a run and Market Man
will appear immediately. Remove the query afterward for normal gameplay.
